# Backlink Targets — August 2026 (Ahrefs competitor-gap edition)

Site: DR 3.6 (up from 1.8 in June), 427 live refdomains. Goal: quality over count now, the head terms need real topical authority, not more low-DR citations.

Sources: Ahrefs referring-domain pulls for secondwindconsultants.com, delanceystreet.com, nationalcreditpartners.com (2026-08-04). Targets below are links competitors actually hold, so they are proven attainable in this niche.

## Tier 1 — Industry pubs (the links that actually move head terms)

1. **deBanked** — debanked.com (DR 61) — THE MCA industry trade publication; NCP holds 4 links. Angles: (a) pitch the BBB-ratings dataset as a story ("we graded every MCA settlement firm's BBB record"), (b) comment newsworthy settlements/lawsuits they cover, (c) their forum debanked.com/forums has an active MCA section. Action: email tips@ + participate in forum. HIGH priority, most topically relevant link possible.
2. **Overdrive** — overdriveonline.com (DR 72) — trucking industry pub, NCP has 3 links. We have /best-for-trucking-companies. Pitch: trucking-specific MCA debt data/guide ("what owner-operators should know before signing an MCA settlement contract"). Medium.
3. **TechBullion** — techbullion.com (DR 81) — accepts contributed fintech articles, NCP has 2. Pitch a data piece. Easy-Medium.
4. **AFCC** — americanfaircreditcouncil.org (DR 50) — debt-relief trade association; NCP listed. Check membership/directory criteria for review sites or for Coastal itself (link may go to coastaldebt.com, still ecosystem value). Medium.

13. **PR wires** — prnewswire.com/newswire.com show up on both competitors. One well-packaged data release ("State of MCA Settlement 2026: BBB grades of 17 firms") syndicates widely. Medium, costs money, one-time.

## Tier 4 — Data-PR engine (the differentiator; feeds Tiers 1+3)
